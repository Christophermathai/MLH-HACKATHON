# ZoomOut brand guidelines

## Character

ZoomOut feels like a playful civic institution: part court docket, part field
notebook, and always humane. The product should make a disagreement feel smaller
without making either person feel small.

## Visual language

- Use warm paper backgrounds, near-black ink, vermilion evidence marks, and a
  restrained teal for measured or successful states.
- Favor editorial serif headings with Malayalam-capable serif fallbacks and a
  clear sans-serif body with Malayalam-capable fallbacks.
- Use thin ruled lines, numbered evidence labels, stamps, and marginal notes.
- Keep cards squared or softly rounded. Avoid floating glass panels, purple
  gradients, and generic dashboard styling.

## Voice

- Clear, lightly playful, and never mocking.
- Call test data evidence, runs passes, and unresolved inputs blockers.
- Clearly label simulated output and never present it as model evidence.

## Accessibility

- Maintain strong contrast and visible focus states.
- Do not rely on color alone for run state.
- Malayalam text needs generous line height and must never be uppercased.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences.
